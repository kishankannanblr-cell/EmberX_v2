EmberX_v2 – Tactical Rescue Triage

AI-Powered Human Detection & Room-Priority Rescue Triage in High-Rise Building Fires

Project Guardian X
Abstract

High-rise building fires pose extreme hazards, forcing search-and-rescue (SAR) firefighters to make split-second triage decisions amidst heavy smoke opacity and structural debris. **EmberX_v2** introduces an offline tactical edge triage model that analyzes multi-room video feeds in real-time. By leveraging a custom **YOLOv8s-OBB** architecture to detect fire, smoke, and trapped victims simultaneously, it automates a 6-tier triage routing algorithm, directing responders to critical threats first and minimizing life-safety risks.

Mission Objectives
  • Target Detection: Deploy localized edge inference to detect fires, smoke plumes, and humans.
  • Automated Prioritization: Run an algorithmic 6-tier matrix to rank room-by-room rescue urgency.
  • Offline Integrity: Operate completely local, bypassing internet constraints on high-rise incident sites.

Multi-Room Surveillance

Captures simultaneous video streams from multiple GoPro and bodycam feeds across building floors.

Edge YOLO Prioritization

Performs instant OBB object detection (Fire/Human/Smoke) and prioritizes active rooms offline.

Research & Methodology
1. Research

Explored YOLO limitations in smoke-filled, dense scenarios.

2. Dataset

Sourced and annotated fireground images with OBB targets.

3. Develop

Trained OBB models and created local web APIs.

4. Testing

Validated pipeline on active fires via local test streams.
